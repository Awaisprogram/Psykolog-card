Who we are

Online Psychologist help with a short meeting time.

Psykologi is the science of the mind and human behaviour. We believe that every person deserves fast, affordable access to a licensed online psychologist. 

Our platform connects you with experienced psychologists who specialise in a wide range of mental health areas. Whether you prefer to speak with a psychologist from the comfort of your home or visit our clinic.

Sessions are available during the day, evening, and weekends, so you can always find a time that fits your life.

What we treat

Mental Health Conditions Our Psychologists Treat.

Our psychologist has broad expertise across all common mental health conditions. Whether you are facing anxiety for the first time or have lived with depression for years, we have a psychologist specialist who can help.

How we help

Psychological therapies.

Our psychologists draw on a broad range of recognised methods — choosing the approach, or blend of approaches, that fits you best.

Not sure which is right for you? Your psychologist will recommend an approach in your first session.
How it works

A psychologist without a referral, in three steps.

Our experienced psychologists provide compassionate care that fits your life — online, by phone or in person.

1 · Choose a psychologist

Find a psychologist who matches your needs. Read about their experience, specialisation and treatment areas — then pick the one who feels right.

2 · Book an appointment

Choose a time that suits you — day, evening or weekend. You can easily book a psychologist online, with no referral required and a short waiting time.

3 · Speak & follow up

Speak with an experienced psychologist online or in person. Receive professional support tailored to your needs, with continued follow-up afterwards.
